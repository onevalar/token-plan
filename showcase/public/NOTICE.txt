`platforms/tauri/src/components/arc/`. See the kit's [`README`](../../README.md)
for every adapter change and the remaining source-file mappings.

The following provenance and adaptation descriptions are retained from that
commit's `components/arc/NOTICE.md` (unrelated component notices are omitted):

- Foundation and motion tokens were retrieved by TokenPlan on **2026-10-20**:
  <https://uiarc.dev/r/arc-foundation.json> and
  <https://uiarc.dev/r/arc-motion-tokens.json>.
- Gauge and WaffleChart were retrieved on **2026-10-22**:
  <https://uiarc.dev/r/gauge.json> and
  <https://uiarc.dev/r/waffle-chart.json>.
  These read-only quota adaptations have deliberately smaller local interfaces,
  not the upstream component APIs. Gauge retains Arc's 270-degree geometry,
  proportions and central readout. Arc/ARIA ranges are bounded, while displayed
  numbers and accessible descriptions preserve overflow. WaffleChart retains
  largest-remainder allocation, bottom-left column fill, 3px gaps and cell shape
  for 100 cells. Missing/out-of-range readings do not mount a grid. Interactive
  legends, tooltips, initial animation, count-up and per-cell motion are omitted.
- Additional Home styles reuse the same Gauge geometry for compact and independent
  concentric meters. Ruler and Segmented Bar adapt the free Usage Meter's track,
  `scaleX` fill and limit marker (<https://uiarc.dev/r/usage-meter.json>); the last
  segment is partially filled. Numbers uses the free Metric Card's static
  label/value/context structure (<https://uiarc.dev/r/metric-card.json>).
  These are local read-only quota presentations, not upstream variant APIs.
- ExpandableCard was retrieved on **2026-10-22**:
  <https://uiarc.dev/r/expandable-card.json>. It retains native buttons, inert
  closed panels, Escape focus return, reduced-motion support, Lucide icons and
  the existing motion tokens. Benefits without expiry dates stay plain rows.
- StaticDonut adapts the free Donut Chart's cumulative-angle annular sectors:
  <https://uiarc.dev/r/donut-chart.json>. It keeps a fixed recorded-token denominator
  without hidden-category renormalization, hover resizing, tooltips or animation.
  Exact readings and zero-token models remain in the legend; invalid or overly
  fragmented data retains the original list.

## 2026-10-24 additions from the software feature

- `sparkline/static-sparkline.tsx` retains the source app's static adaptation of
